# ESO account companion

## Language

**Character**: A game character identified within an account and server. A name is a display label that can change, not its identity.

**Account inventory**: Owned item stacks across characters and shared storage on one account/server. A spouse's separate account remains a separate ownership pool.

**Observation**: Information last recorded about the game, with its origin and available timestamps. It is not necessarily the current live state.

**Character state**: The character's observed progression and applied configuration assembled from available sources. Sections may have different scan times; a saved proposal is not observed applied state.

**Account**: Characters and shared possessions belonging to one ESO account on one server.

**Progression**: Earned levels, skill and morph progress, unlocks and crafting knowledge, distinct from current point allocation.

**Build draft**: A named editable proposal for a character's configuration. It remains separate from the setup observed in game.

**Target requirement**: A condition needed for a desired build, such as possessing an item, unlocking a skill or applying an allocation. Priority, optionality and prerequisite relationships are separate properties of the requirement.

**Application verification**: Comparison of a proposed configuration with subsequently observed game state. Generating or importing a profile does not establish application.

**Source coverage**: Which character, storage and data sections a source observed, their completeness and available scan times.

**Research summary**: Recorded trait counts and display labels by craft/research line, with active timers and their scan time. It does not supply numeric trait IDs or confirm learning when a timer expires.

**Saved build**: A planned or saved allocation of skills, attributes, equipment and champion points. Its presence does not establish that it is applied.

**Knowledge**: A character's observed recipe, motif, furnishing, scribing or research progress. Missing observations are distinct from an observed negative result.

**Collection**: Account-wide set-piece unlocks, independent of whether a corresponding physical item is owned.

**Catalog**: Shared definitions describing game IDs, sets, items and skills. It contains no account ownership information.
